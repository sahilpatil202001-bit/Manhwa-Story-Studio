# Licensing, Rights & Compliance Policy

## Artwork Authorization

Manhwa Story Studio is engineered strictly for authorized media creation.

- **Explicit Rights Declaration Required**: The rendering engine halts if artwork assets carry `unknown` or `prohibited` rights tags.
- **Accepted Statuses for Production**:
  - `owned`
  - `licensed`
  - `permission_granted`
  - `public_domain_verified`
  - `other_authorized_use`
- **No Automated Web Scraping**: The studio does not scrape copyrighted webtoon platforms. Users must provide their own authorized panels.
- **Transformative Storytelling**: The script generator produces concise transformative commentary rather than verbatim translations of copyrighted dialogue.

## Audio & Font Licenses

- **Narration**: Free high-quality Hindi neural synthesis is supported via Edge-TTS or custom recorded voice files.
- **Music & Sound Effects**: Background music must be royalty-free or properly licensed. Place track files in `assets/audio/` and reference them in `project.yaml`.
- **Fonts**: Default font is Arial with fallback. Ensure custom fonts used in ASS subtitles permit video embedding and broadcasting.
